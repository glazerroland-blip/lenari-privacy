Privacy Policy · Last updated: March 2026

Lenari ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information – When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address.
  • Usage data – Study session duration, materials studied, quiz results stored locally on your device.
  • Uploaded content – PDF files you upload are processed on-device and sent to Google AI API for text-to-speech and AI features only.
  • Device information – Basic device info for crash reporting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Lenari app experience
  • To generate AI-powered study summaries and quiz questions via Google AI API
  • To track your study progress and streaks (stored locally)
  • To enable community features (sharing progress with friends)

3. Third-Party Services

Lenari uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AI (Gemini API) – For AI explanations and quiz generation. Your document text is sent to Google's servers for processing. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Google) – For authentication and data sync. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Android Text-to-Speech – Built-in device TTS, no data sent externally.

4. Data Storage

Your study data (progress, exam dates, settings) is stored:

  • Primarily locally on your device
  • If you sign in, synced to Firebase (encrypted, Google-managed servers)
  • We do not sell your data to third parties

5. AI API Data Processing

When you use AI features (explanations, quiz generation, summaries), portions of your document text are sent to Google's Gemini API. This data is:

  • Used only to generate the requested AI response
  • Subject to Google AI Terms of Service
  • Not stored by us beyond the current session

6. Children's Privacy

Lenari is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your study data
  • Opt out of analytics

To exercise these rights, contact us at: lenari.app@gmail.com

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification.
